Air-Taxi Routing Simulator

An AI routing engine assigns timed 4D routes — latitude, longitude, altitude, time — and resolves conflicts as they form.

Concept demo. This simulator uses simplified, fictional routing logic and simulated aircraft and demand only — no live air-traffic data. It is not certified aviation traffic-control software and must not be used for real flight operations.

Routing engine log

    How it works: the engine projects every aircraft forward along its route. When two aircraft at the same flight level are projected to pass within the separation minimum, it flags a conflict and re-plans one of them — a flight-level change, a speed adjustment, or a lateral offset waypoint — then verifies the pair is clear.
